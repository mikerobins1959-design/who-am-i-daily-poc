Your game stays in your browser

You do not need an account. The game saves your daily progress, results, scores and cumulative statistics, including streaks, in your browser’s Local Storage under who-am-i-daily-state. This lets you resume a game and keeps a completed daily result locked. The application does not send this saved game state to us.

Session Storage remembers whether the introduction has played for a date in a browser tab. Your browser normally clears it when that tab’s session ends, although restoring tabs may restore the session.

For offline play, a service worker caches game pages, images, scripts and puzzle data on your device. These caches contain game resources, rather than an uploaded record of your play.

The game code does not set cookies or include advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.

When you visit the site

Netlify hosts the site. Loading pages and game resources sends connection information to the hosting service, such as your IP address, requested URL and browser or device information supplied by your browser. This is used to deliver and protect the site. It is separate from the game state saved on your device.

Netlify’s published Data Processing Agreement describes processing outside the UK and European Economic Area, including in the US, and transfer mechanisms including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework with its UK extension and contractual safeguards. See the Netlify Trust Center for its published supplier information.

Contacting us and sharing results

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to support, feedback and privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your enquiry.

We retain support correspondence while the game remains online. You can contact us to request deletion; we may need to retain information where required by law or to resolve a dispute.

The game information panel also links to Instagram and TikTok. Following those links takes you to an external service, which receives connection information and applies its own privacy policy. These are ordinary links, not embedded social trackers.

Sharing happens only when you choose it. The result text includes the puzzle date, outcome, clue number, score and game URL, without the answer. Your browser may pass this to an app you select or copy it to your clipboard. Any recipient or sharing service handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Removing information from your device

Local game data has no automatic expiry in the application. It stays until you clear it or your browser removes it. Offline resources stay until your browser removes them or a game update replaces them.

To remove stored game data and offline resources, open your browser’s privacy or site settings, find this website and clear its site data or storage. The labels vary by browser. This removes your saved progress and statistics from that browser; they cannot be recovered by us. Merely uninstalling an app shortcut may leave browser site data behind.

Close game tabs before clearing site data. A later visit will download game resources again and create new local progress when you play. Clearing data does not remove emails already sent or results already shared.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict its use, and obtain a portable copy. Contact mickrobins@mac.com to exercise your rights or raise a concern. We cannot retrieve game data held only in your browser.

You also have a right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, where applicable.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if the game’s handling of information changes. The date above shows when this notice was last updated.
