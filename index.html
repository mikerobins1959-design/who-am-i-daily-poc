Frequently asked questions

How does scoring work?

Clue 1 is worth 3 points, clue 2 is worth 2 points, and clue 3 is worth 1 point.

How do I submit an answer?

Select Reveal Multiple Choices to see eight answer options. Revealing the choices locks the current clue, so you cannot reveal another clue before guessing. You have one guess, and the selected option must exactly match the puzzle answer. A wrong answer ends the day with 0 points.

Is there a timer?

No. There is no timer before or after you reveal the choices. You have until local midnight to submit your answer.

When does the puzzle change?

The daily puzzle changes at midnight according to your browser’s local date. An unfinished game is not recorded as a completed game or loss when the new day begins.

What happens if I refresh or open another tab?

Your current clue and any revealed choices are preserved in this browser until local midnight, so you can continue the same daily game.

How are streaks and statistics saved?

A streak counts consecutive daily wins. Your streak and statistics are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing this site’s browser data resets your game history, streak, and statistics.

How do I add the game to my iPhone or iPad?

Open the game in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
